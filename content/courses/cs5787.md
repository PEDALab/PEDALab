This course covers advanced topics in computer vision with a specific focus on applications in agriculture. Students will learn about image processing, feature extraction, object recognition, and deep learning techniques as they apply to various agricultural challenges. Topics include:

*   **Crop Phenotyping**: Automated measurement of plant traits from images.
*   **Disease and Pest Detection**: Identifying plant diseases and pests using visual data.
*   **Yield Estimation**: Predicting crop yields from aerial and ground-based imagery.
*   **Robotics in Agriculture**: Vision-guided robots for precision farming tasks.

Prerequisites: CS 4780 (Machine Learning for Data Science) or equivalent.
